Privacy Policy

For The Context Dictionary (Android, package com.context.dict.v1)
Effective date: 18 July 2026 · Last updated: 18 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information The Context Dictionary (the “App”, “we”, “us”), published by NeuroDev Labs [CONFIRM: full legal entity name], handles when you use it, and your choices. By using the App you agree to this Policy.

The short version. The App has no accounts and no login. Your saved words, search history, and settings stay on your device. When you look up a word, that word (and any style/domain you pick) is sent to our AI and reference providers to generate a result. We show ads and sell an optional subscription, which involve trusted third parties (Google and RevenueCat). We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

NeuroDev Labs — “Building tools for curious minds.” Contact: support@neurodevlabs.com. [CONFIRM: postal address if required in your region.]

2. Information the App handles

a) Stored only on your device

  • Saved words & definitions — your personal library (stored locally in an on-device database).
  • Recent search history — your most recent lookups (stored locally).
  • App settings & usage counters — daily free-search count, onboarding state, notification schedule, and whether premium is active (stored locally).

This information is not transmitted to us. It is removed when you clear it in the App or uninstall the App.

b) Sent to third parties to make the App work

WhatWhere it goesWhy
The word or phrase you look up (plus the persona/domain/mode you choose) Google (Gemini / Generative Language API); Wikipedia (REST API); dictionaryapi.dev To generate the definition, cultural translation, and to fact-check it against reference sources.
Advertising & device identifiers, approximate ad-interaction data Google AdMob To show rewarded ads (free tier) and measure/limit them.
Purchase & subscription events, an anonymous app-user ID Google Play Billing and RevenueCat To process the optional subscription, validate it, and restore purchases.

We do not attach your name, email, or account to these requests — the App has no account system. Please review the providers’ own policies: Google, RevenueCat, Wikimedia.

3. What we do not collect

No name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or account credentials. The App has no sign-up. We do not build advertising profiles ourselves, and we do not sell personal information.

The free tier uses Google AdMob rewarded ads. AdMob may use advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. Where required (e.g. the EEA/UK), a consent prompt governs personalised ads, and you can reset or limit your advertising ID in Android Settings. Subscribing removes ads.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Data retention & deletion

On-device data persists until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. Requests sent to the providers above are retained under their policies. Because there is no account, there is no server-side profile of you for us to delete; to remove all local data, clear the App’s storage or uninstall it. For any questions, email support@neurodevlabs.com.
